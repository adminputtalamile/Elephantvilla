Travel Guide

How To Get To Komari

Directions for reaching Elephant Beach Villa from Colombo or Arugam Bay.

🚌 By Bus from Colombo

The direct private bus service from Colombo passes directly along the Selvapuram village road in Komari, which leads straight to the property gate.

🛺 By Tuk-Tuk from Arugam Bay / Pottuvil

Food City supermarket is a 20-minute drive away in Pottuvil town. From the villa, 3-wheel 'tuk tuk' transport can easily be arranged to all surf points and national parks.
