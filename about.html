Section 2
The Villa Space & Amenities

Designed for Total Privacy & Comfort

Elephant Beach Villa is a rare alternative for travellers who want to experience Sri Lanka’s eastern wild coast at its absolute best. Positioned between a pristine, uncrowded beach with rolling sand dunes on one side and a scenic lagoon/estuary on the other.

The villa features 4 private air-conditioned bedrooms, each equipped with its own hot water ensuite bathroom, ceiling fan, and private balcony offering views of the ocean or estuary.

Guests have access to a fully kitted main kitchen (stove, oven/grill, microwave, fridge-freezer, rice cooker, kettle) on the ground floor, as well as a separate upstairs kitchenette for beverages. Enjoy dining at the cozy upstairs living room or on the spacious downstairs verandah featuring 2 outdoor dining areas.

Section 4
Explore Eastern Sri Lanka

Surrounding Attractions & Excursions

From world-renowned surfing breaks to wild elephant safaris, our Komari location puts you at the heart of adventure.

World-Class Surf Points

Located just 15 minutes away from Lighthouse Point, Whiskey Point, and Pottuvil Point—renowned spots for all surfing levels.

3 National Parks (1h 15m)

Easy day trips to East Yala (Kumana) for bird life, Lahugala for elephant herds, and Gal Oya for boat safaris over Parakrama Samudraya Lake.

Local Transport & Cafes

Situated in quiet Komari village, 20 mins from Arugam Bay main strip. Local cafes are within 10 mins walk, and 3-wheel 'tuk tuk' service is easily arranged.
