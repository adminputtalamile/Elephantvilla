Section 2
Why Choose Elephant Beach

Unmatched Natural Luxury

Located in the quiet village of Komari, just 20 minutes from main Arugam Bay, offering a private paradise away from the crowd.

Pristine Deserted Beach

Steps away from wide, uncrowded sandy beaches with direct ocean access, perfect for sunrise walks.

Visiting Wild Elephants

Situated in a rich natural corridor where gentle wild elephants frequently roam the nearby lagoon and dunes.

Private Plunge Pool

A warm, large private pool set in lush tropical gardens, ideal for cooling off after a day of ocean surfing.

15 Mins to World Surf Points

Minutes away from world-famous surf breaks including Lighthouse Point, Whiskey Point, and Pottuvil Point.
